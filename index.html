FAQ

Questions business owners ask.

Do I need a computer?

No. Ledger works on a phone, and also on a tablet or laptop. Your records are the same on every device you sign in on.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once it has been opened on your device. You can keep recording sales offline. Ledger saves them on the device and syncs when you are back online.

How do my staff sign in?

You connect each device to your business account once. After that, staff just pick their name and enter their own PIN. You decide what they can see.

Is my data safe?

Each business account is private, and your records are saved to the cloud. We never ask for your card, bank details or Mobile Money PIN.

Can I run more than one business?

Yes. You can run up to 3 businesses under one login. Each one keeps its own sales, stock, customers and staff. The extra ones are GH₵30 a month each.

How do I add Ledger to my home screen?
  1. iPhone (Safari): open Ledger, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android (Chrome): open Ledger, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

It then opens full screen like any other app and stays signed in.

How do I pay?

With Mobile Money. Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you activate your account.
